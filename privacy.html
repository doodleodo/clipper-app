Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Clipper is a private tool run by its owner. Contact: github.com/doodleodo

2. What data Clipper uses

  • When the owner connects a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, Clipper receives an access token and basic account information (such as the account ID and name) from that platform.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions the owner chooses to publish.
  • Information the platform returns after publishing (such as the post ID and status).

3. Why

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts and to show whether a post was successful.

4. Where it is stored

Data is stored on the owner's private server. Access tokens are kept secret and are never shared.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold and not shared with anyone, except with the platform the video is published to.

6. How long

Access tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account. Disconnecting is possible at any time in the account settings of each platform.

7. Other people

Clipper is not offered to other users and does not collect data about other people.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always on this page.
